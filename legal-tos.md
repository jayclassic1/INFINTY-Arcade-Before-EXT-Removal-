## 5. Platform Credits

### 5.1 Tokens
- Tokens are bought by depositing ICP into your Arcade Wallet and converting it at the Platform's fixed rate (currently **1 ICP = 100 Tokens**). Conversion is one-way: Tokens cannot be converted back into ICP, withdrawn, or redeemed for money.
- Tokens are spent to play games, to buy a copy of a purchasable game, and to tip game creators.
- Tokens may be sent to other users through the Platform's Send Tokens feature, subject to a daily limit (currently **1,000 Tokens per day**). Sending is final; we cannot reverse a transfer sent to the wrong user.
- ICP you have deposited but not yet converted to Tokens remains withdrawable from your Arcade Wallet, less the ICP network fee.

### 5.2 Tickets
- Tickets are won by playing Ticket Games (Section 6). They cannot be bought, sent to other users, or exchanged for ICP or money.
- Tickets are spent in the Prize Booth to redeem NFTs. The Platform values Tickets for redemption at a fixed rate (currently **1,000 Tickets = 1 ICP** of backing), which determines the Ticket price of Prize Booth items.
- Tickets have no use or value outside the Platform.

### 5.3 General
- Exchange rates may change for new deposits and new games with notice on the Platform. Changes are not retroactive to credits already held.
- Credits are a limited, revocable licence to use Platform features. You do not own them. We may adjust, reset or discontinue them with reasonable notice, and may remove credits obtained through cheating, exploits or error without notice.

## 6. Ticket Games, Prizes and Jackpots

Some games on the Platform are **Ticket Games**: games where a Token is spent to play and Tickets are awarded based on the player's performance.

### 6.1 Skill, not chance
Tickets in Ticket Games are awarded solely on the player's score or performance, as determined by the game's own rules and verified by the Platform. No element of chance decides whether a play wins Tickets.

### 6.2 Ticket pools
Each Ticket Game has a Ticket pool funded from a fixed share of the Tokens spent on that game (currently 80% of each play), plus any reserve the operator chooses to add. Tickets can only be paid from that game's pool. If a pool is empty, a play may earn fewer Tickets or none, regardless of score. Pool status is shown on the game card.

### 6.3 Jackpots and Breakers
- A Ticket Game may pay a **jackpot** of additional Tickets for a new all-time record on that game. Jackpots are capped per win (currently 25 Tickets) and are only available to players who hold DAO membership (Section 11). Record-setting plays by non-members still count as records but earn no jackpot.
- A player who holds a record may be designated a **Breaker** and retired from that game's record and jackpot eligibility. Their score remains as a ceiling to beat. They become eligible again once another jackpot-eligible player beats that score. Breaker designation is at the operator's discretion and is not a penalty.
- Jackpot and record rules may change for future plays with notice.

### 6.4 Score verification
Scores are submitted by the game to the Platform under a per-game signing key and are checked for plausibility. Scores the Platform cannot verify, or that are implausible for the game, are held for review and may be rejected. A rejected score earns no Tickets and no record. The operator's decision on a held score is final.

### 6.5 No cash prizes
Tickets, jackpots and records never pay ICP or money to players. The only thing Tickets can be exchanged for is Prize Booth NFTs.
