## 3. What Is Personal Information

Under PIPA, "personal information" means information about an identifiable individual. On a blockchain-based platform this is nuanced:

- **Internet Identity principal IDs** are pseudonymous identifiers. We treat them as personal information because, in some circumstances, they may be linkable to a real person.
- **Display names and bios** you voluntarily set are personal information.
- **On-chain transaction data** (token balances, gameplay records, NFT ownership) is recorded on a public blockchain and is outside our control once written. We cannot delete blockchain records.

## 4. Information We Collect

### 4.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Display name and bio** - set voluntarily in your profile
- **Profile picture** - an NFT image you select from your wallet
- **Forum and chat posts** - content you post in community features
- **Game submissions** - game name, URL, description, and developer name you provide

### 4.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Internet Identity principal ID** - a pseudonymous identifier created when you authenticate; required to operate your account
- **ICP wallet account ID** - a derived identifier used for token deposits; no private keys are ever collected
- **Gameplay activity** - plays, scores, token and ticket transactions, checkpoint saves; stored on-chain
- **Session activity** - views, clicks, and interactions within the Platform; used for Platform operation only
- **Platform credits** - token and ticket balances, transaction history; stored on-chain

### 4.3 Information We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect your real name, address, phone number, or government ID
- We do not collect payment card information (ICP deposits are peer-to-peer blockchain transfers)
- We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising
- We do not use third-party advertising networks
- We do not collect biometric data

## 7. Blockchain Data

Certain information is written to the Internet Computer blockchain as part of normal Platform operation:

- Token and ticket transaction records
- NFT ownership and transfer records
- Governance votes and proposals
- Game scores and leaderboard entries

**This data is permanent and cannot be deleted.** Blockchain records are immutable by design. If you request deletion of your account, we can delete off-chain data (display name, bio, profile picture) but cannot alter blockchain records. Please be aware of this before participating.

## 10. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

- Decentralized storage of on-chain data on the Internet Computer (managed by DFINITY Foundation)
- No collection of private keys or seed phrases
- Access controls limiting who can access administrative functions
- Platform smart contracts audited before handling material user funds

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a privacy breach that poses real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta (OIPC) as required by PIPA.
